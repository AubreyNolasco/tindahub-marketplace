# JOM HUB — User Manual (Merchant & Reseller)

This manual covers day-to-day use of JOM HUB for the two account types that run a business on the platform. For platform-wide concepts (roles, fees, order lifecycle), read the [System Overview](./SYSTEM_OVERVIEW.md) first. If something isn't working the way this manual describes, check the [Troubleshooting Guide](./TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

---

## Part 1 — Merchant

### 1.1 Getting started

1. Sign up with your email (or Google) and verify the 6-digit OTP.
2. Choose **Merchant** and complete your business profile. You land in your dashboard immediately — you can look around right away, but posting products stays locked until the steps below are done.
3. Upload a clear, valid business permit from your dashboard. New Merchants start on a **free 6-month Starter subscription** — no payment needed to begin. You can upgrade to a longer paid plan (Growth · 1 Year, Pro · 2 Years) any time before it lapses.
4. Admin reviews your permit and your subscription separately. Once both are approved, your store activates. If you need to start selling before your permit review finishes, you can request **temporary access** — Admin can grant a time-boxed grace period from the Merchant Follow-Ups flow.

### 1.2 Choosing your service type

Under **Store Settings → Service Type**, choose what kind of business you run:

- **Product Store** — sell physical products to Resellers through the catalog.
- **Clinic Services** — offer dental, optical, or medical services; Resellers refer customers to you and earn a referral fee per completed service.
- **Real Estate Services** — list property-related services the same way; Resellers refer prospective buyers/tenants for a referral fee.
- **Product Store + Services** — run both at once.

Changing this later is safe — it only affects how Resellers can interact with your store going forward.

### 1.3 Listing a product

From **Products → Add Product**:

- Enter a truthful name, description, category, real product image, and stock count.
- Set your **retail price**, **wholesale price** (must be below retail), a **suggested customer price**, and any bulk quantity tiers.
- Keep at least **15% projected gross margin** for the Reseller — the system checks this.
- Add minimum order quantity, packed measurements, and any special handling notes.
- Prohibited, illegal, counterfeit, unsafe, or misleading products cannot be posted — listings are screened automatically and can be rejected on review.

### 1.4 Fulfilling an order

1. When an order comes in, move it from **Confirmed** to **Processing** and pack the exact items ordered.
2. Enter the **actual courier fee** for the Reseller to confirm — you can't dispatch until they accept it.
3. If the Reseller declines the fee (with a required note), you can revise the courier or the amount and resubmit. Nothing ships on a declined fee.
4. Once the fee is accepted, enter courier, tracking number, delivery schedule, and dispatch proof, then mark it **Shipped**.
5. The Reseller confirms receipt, or opens a dispute if there's a problem. If no dispute is opened, the order **auto-completes 7 days after the estimated delivery date**, and your payout (minus the 3% success fee) posts automatically.

### 1.5 Cancellations, disputes, and refunds

Either side can open a cancellation, dispute, return, replacement, or refund case with a reason — this pauses automatic completion. You can review and resolve an early cancellation yourself; Admin steps in for escalated or already-shipped-order cases. An approved cancellation reverses the wallet charge, the Reseller fee, and restores stock, exactly once.

### 1.6 Wallet, payouts, and withdrawals

Your payouts land in your Merchant wallet. To withdraw: minimum ₱500 per withdrawal, a ₱100,000 daily limit, and a 24-hour wait after changing your payout destination (for safety). Admin schedules the transfer and uploads proof once sent.

### 1.7 Reports

From **Reports**, generate Sales, Inventory, Top-Up, Withdrawal, or Order reports for a chosen date range (Today, Last 7/30 Days, custom, etc.) and download as Excel. Use **Print Receipt** on an individual order/top-up/withdrawal record when you need a single printable copy.

### 1.8 Getting help

Ask **JOM Bits** (the in-app assistant, bottom-right on most pages) any question about your workflow — it answers based on how JOM HUB actually works, in whichever language you ask in.

## Part 2 — Reseller

### 2.1 Getting started

1. Sign up with your email (or Google) and verify the 6-digit OTP.
2. Choose **Reseller** and complete your contact and delivery address. You land in your dashboard immediately — no wallet top-up required just to finish signing up.
3. When you're ready to buy, verify your identity and submit an initial wallet top-up from your dashboard. Admin reviews both independently; placing orders unlocks once they're approved.

### 2.2 Buying from the catalog

Browse the Merchant catalog and open a product's details to see pricing:

- **Reseller price** is used before retail price whenever it's lower.
- Buying in bulk can unlock a lower **quantity-tier price** — it can only reduce your buying price, never increase it.
- Before checkout, you set your own **customer selling price**; the system shows your projected profit after the Reseller fee (1% of product total, ₱3 minimum, ₱50 maximum per order).
- Checkout always re-quotes price and stock from the server, and only debits your wallet if everything (price, stock, your access, your balance) is still valid at that moment.

### 2.3 Managing customers and orders

Save your customers under **Customers** so you can quickly select them when placing an order or making a referral. Get their consent before recording their contact and delivery details. When placing an order on their behalf: pick one piece or a bulk quantity, confirm the price and delivery details, then complete checkout.

### 2.4 Your customer storefront

Under **My Product List**, pick specific Merchant products you want to sell — only what you explicitly select appears on your storefront. Set a unique store name, profile photo, cover photo, an introduction, and optional contact channels (Facebook, phone, Viber, WhatsApp). Share your readable store-name link; when a customer wants to buy, they tap **How to Buy** and reach you through your published contact channel. You then confirm the product, quantity, final price, delivery, shipping fee, and payment method with them directly and record the order yourself. Your storefront never exposes JOM HUB's main navigation, your address, wallet, or payout details to customers.

### 2.5 Referral services (clinics & real estate)

Under **Services & Providers**, browse partner clinics and real-estate service providers and their listed services. Tap **Refer Customer**, pick a saved customer (or enter their details manually), and submit. The provider follows up with your customer directly; once they confirm the service is completed, your referral fee is transferred to your wallet automatically — no upfront cost to you.

### 2.6 Delivery (Lalamove)

Under **Delivery Settings**, connect your own Lalamove account to get real-time delivery quotes on your orders (Metro Manila and Cebu areas are supported).

### 2.7 Wallet and withdrawals

Track your wallet balance, top-ups, and order spending from **Wallet**. Withdrawals follow the same rules as Merchants: ₱500 minimum, ₱100,000 daily limit, 24-hour wait after changing payout details.

### 2.8 Reports

Same reporting tools as Merchants — Sales, Order, Top-Up, and Withdrawal reports by date range, downloadable as Excel, plus per-record printable receipts.

### 2.9 Getting help

Ask **JOM Bits** for guidance on any Reseller workflow — products, orders, wallet, referrals — it answers in the language you ask in, whether or not the AI-assisted mode is enabled.
