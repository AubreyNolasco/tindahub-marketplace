# JOM HUB — Frequently Asked Questions

These are the same questions answered on the public homepage FAQ section, plus a few more common ones. For step-by-step procedures, see the [User Manual](./USER_MANUAL.md) or [Admin Guide](./ADMIN_GUIDE.md).

### How do I get started as a Merchant?
Sign up with your Gmail, enter the 6-digit OTP, and choose the "Merchant" role. Complete your business details and you'll land in your dashboard right away with a free 6-month subscription, no payment needed to start. Upload a valid business permit — posting products stays locked until Admin approves it, or grants you temporary access on request while it's under review. Renew anytime from your dashboard before your free 6 months ends to keep it active.

### How do I get started as a Reseller?
Sign up with your Gmail, enter the 6-digit OTP, and choose the "Reseller" role. Complete your contact and delivery address and you'll land in your dashboard right away — no wallet top-up required to finish signing up. Verify your identity and top up your wallet whenever you're ready; placing orders unlocks once Admin approves both.

### How does the Clinic Referral System work?
Go to the clinics page, choose a partner dental or optical clinic and their service, then refer your customer by entering their details. Once the appointment is confirmed by the clinic, the referral fee is automatically transferred to your wallet.

### How does the Real Estate referral service work?
Browse partner real-estate service providers on the same Services & Providers page used for clinics, then refer your customer to them. Once the service they're offering is completed, you receive a referral fee — at no upfront cost to you. (A Merchant enables this by setting their service type to "Real Estate Services" in Store Settings.)

### What are the payment options?
Use JOM HUB's InstaPay QR to top up your wallet. Scan the QR, enter the amount, and upload your payment screenshot along with the one-use reference number. Admin reviews the payment before your wallet is credited.

### How does Lalamove delivery work?
Merchants connect their Lalamove account in Delivery Settings. When a Reseller places an order, they get a real-time delivery quote. Metro Manila and Cebu areas are supported.

### What does the Reseller pay in fees?
A capped system fee of 1% of the product total per order, with a ₱3 minimum and a ₱50 maximum — never more than ₱50 regardless of order size.

### What does the Merchant pay in fees?
A 3% success fee on completed product orders only. It's deducted from the payout, not billed separately, and only applies once an order actually completes (not on cancelled or disputed-and-refunded orders).

### What are the Merchant subscription plans?
Starter (6 months, ₱1,599), Growth (1 year, ₱2,799 — best value), and Pro (2 years, ₱4,999). New Merchants start on a free 6-month period and can upgrade at any time.

### How long do I have to dispute an order after it's marked delivered?
An order auto-completes 7 days after its estimated delivery date if no dispute, return, replacement, or refund case is open. Opening a case before that window closes pauses automatic completion.

### Can I change my Reseller storefront's contact details later?
Yes — your storefront (store name, photos, intro, contact channels, and selected products) can be edited any time from **My Product List** / storefront settings. Removing unavailable products and outdated contact channels promptly is recommended.

### Is my activity tracked?
Sensitive account and transaction changes are recorded in an Activity Audit log that Admin can review — this protects everyone by making approvals, rejections, and status changes traceable. Private proofs, passwords, and OTPs are never stored in that log.
