This is a **consistency/cleanup pass**, not a rebuild — real ad-hoc-vs-shared-component inconsistencies were found with exact file:line citations (see plan file), and the work is sequenced by risk (buttons → tables → modals) across 171 `.jsx` files total, executed in small reviewable batches, not one giant sweep.
